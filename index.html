Our Story

How The Sketcherz started

I started The Sketcherz in Ujjain while I was doing my BBA at Vikram University. I kept meeting fine arts students who were very good at their art, but had no place to show it. Their work stayed in their bags and their rooms.

So I built a platform where they could teach, show their work and earn from it. Today The Sketcherz runs from Delhi NCR. We teach at your home, online on live video, and in our studio.

We follow one rule on purpose: the person teaching you must be a trained artist first.

Questions

Things people ask us

Who teaches at The Sketcherz?

Our instructors are BFA graduates. Each one teaches the medium they practise themselves.

How much do classes cost?

Home classes start at Rs 1,999 per session. Live online sessions start at Rs 999 per session. Studio batches run from Rs 999 to Rs 3,299 per month.

Do I need drawing experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Your instructor checks your level in the first class and starts from there.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. Try your first class before you pay for anything.
